Recently I have been trying to get my thoughts in order. I have never been very good at journaling. I have tried dream journaling, daily Obsidian notes, just writing down my thoughts and gratitude journaling. None of them have stuck.

I want a method that I can use to capture what I need in the moment and nothing more. It would be ideal if it took no extra time out of my day, or at least as little as possible, was unintrusive, and simple. I have recently found taking notes has cleared my mind up a lot, as well as not taking medication that was clearly doing me harm but let's not get into that right now. A colleague has been [[Sources/Books/Notes/The Bullet Journal Method\|Bullet Journaling]] and this seemed like a good method to make this process more manageable, but it had some issues for me personally. So I am going to record here my adapted journaling methodology.

## Philosophy 
- Keep notes as short as possible while still being able to understand them
- Have enough detail than on return the note is still useful
## Key for Individual Notes
- [ ] Boxes are used for tasks (This makes more sense to my brain than dots) - ticked when completed, crossed out when dropped, arrows if moved
-- Dashes are used for facts
= equals signs are used for emotions or feelings

## Index
- Kept in the back of the book (this makes more sense to me as it allows the index to grow)
- Record page contents and number

## Monthly Log
- At the start of each month create a two page spread
- The first page should have a list of each date with the initial of the day of the week next to it. Use this to record events on days and to write down what is planned on them
- The second page is a list of tasks for the month

## Monthly Migration
- When you create the monthly log, leaf through the pages of the previous month and migrate any uncompleted tasks or strike them through. Many may be irrelevant
- If moved forward to the monthly log or a collection put a > in the box
- If planned for a future date put a < in the box and add to the future log
- Also check the future log for any tasks that may need migrating to this month

## Notebook Migration
- Evaluate more carefully what to migrate to the new notebook. Collections, tasks etc

## Future Log
- At the start of the notebook have a two page spread
- Divide in to six
- Title each with a month
- Record things happening and tasks for each month

## Reflection
### Morning
- With breakfast write down any thoughts bubbling up
- Review Pages of current month and check open tasks
### Evening
- Before bed scan what you have logged through the day
- Tick off completed tasks
- Write down anything you may have missed
- Assess why things are important and if they are a priority
- Cross out tasks you think are irrelevant
- No screens between evening and morning reflections
