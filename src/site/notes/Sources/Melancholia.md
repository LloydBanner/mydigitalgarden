This film certainly matches the title. It did make me feel melancholy. It is undoubtedly well shot and timed, I also believe it conveys the emotions it intended too perfectly. I also think Justine's sadness and difficulties with life were expressed well. Yet it finishes with Justine being accepting that the world is ending because she believes that life on earth is evil and Claire being upset because her son will die. In the situation there would be nothing you could do and yet I feel the film is overly nihilistic. What is the message, if not a negative one about humanity?
