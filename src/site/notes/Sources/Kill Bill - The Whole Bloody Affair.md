I first watched volume 1 of Kill Bill about ten years ago and didn't think much of it, but never got around to volume 2. I have since watch pretty much every other [[Sources/Quentin Tarantino\|Quentin Tarantino]] film. I have to say I enjoyed this way more the second time around. Volume 1 has so much style and references so many things. I remember thinking the fight at the end of volume 1 was a bit corny the first time around, but I enjoyed that this time. Volume 2 complements the action of volume 1 quite well by being much more dialogue heavy. All in all a great film, I would say probably my fourth favourite Tarantino film currently (08-11-2026) after [[Sources/Django Unchained\|Django Unchained]], [[Sources/Inglourious Basterds\|Inglourious Basterds]], and [[Sources/Pulp Fiction\|Pulp Fiction]].
