I don't think the description for this film doe sit justice. This may be one of the best sci-fi films I have ever watched. It has so much charm and character and is both genuinely funny and emotional. I went to see it with my dad on some free tickets that he had and I think we were both very pleasantly surprised.

A science teacher wakes up alone on a spaceship. As his memory returns, he uncovers a mission to stop a mysterious substance killing Earth's sun and that an unexpected friendship may be the key.
