I have been lacking direction recently and think I have fallen into a bad habit of consuming a lot of media. There are lots of things that I want to do that are more creative that I now never seem to get around to. I have lost hours to things like YouTube videos and not got that much out of them. I want a more considered approach to media consumption so I have decided to write about every piece of media I consume here. This will make me think twice about mindlessly consuming as there will be more effort in doing so. I have been reading [[Sources/Books/Notes/The Bullet Journal Method\|The Bullet Journal Method]] recently and a lot of the concepts behind it are that you will remove things from you to-do lists because of the effort of rewriting them. This is a similar concept. I think the effort of having to write about something after you have done it will create some extra resistance to doing the thing. This is especially so for short form media like YouTube videos because you would have to write more if you spent them same amount of time watching YouTube videos vs films. I think this works well because I would rather spend more time on longer form content such as films or books, because ultimately I get more out of them. I have gained much less from YouTube videos than films or books and yet have likely spent more time watching them. I intend to change this.

Now I need some more rules:
- What about video games? They can go on forever. I could have written about a video game and just keep playing it and spend all my time doing that. In that case I should write a full essay on the game in question.
- What If I rewatch something? Write about it again, has your opinion changed. Compare your experiences or write about anything different that you felt.
- How much should you write? The writing must not be mindless, it must be considered. It should be enough to have considered the ideas expressed and your opinions of them. Never less than a handful of sentences but often more.
