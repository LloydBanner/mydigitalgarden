## Session Notes

**[[TTRPG/Elbon Setting/Calendar/Elbon Calendar\|Calendar]] Date:** 43-3-596 - 46-3-596

### The Return to Stonehaven
- Just as the guards were lining up with the merchants ready to attack the thieves and take back Whiterock. Gary the goblin came running. He clutched Prospero's Eye and shouted "Quick, quick. Stonehaven needs your help. It's under siege from orcs, there were ogres and trolls too. I think I even saw a giant. I don't know how long we can hold out for."
- Eadan turned to the party and told then they had done enough to help and that the merchants and guards could take it from here and stop the thieves. He lent them 5 horses, which they had to share between them. Gary rode with Jatumal, Geog was with Breadwig and Magma used his pigeon ring and flew alongside the others. Eadan also offered to support them in their fight when they had things under control in Whiterock.
- They rode hard toward Stonehaven through the Howling Woods, past the Tarrasque corpse, through the gash of debris caused by the Tarrasque, through the beginnings of the rocky slopes and reached the Pond of Foresight not long after midnight. 
- They heard war drums from the orcs echoing over the slopes, but these fell silent as they reached the pond. In the pond they saw something floating, it had two arrows in its back and turned out to be Zuchi the Kappa. They all took a moment and spared a thought for him. He had been slain by orc arrows.
- At the pond they each took a sip. Breadwig and Jatumal foresaw Pavnutia the vampire cackling in a dungeon flanked by four hellhounds. Magma saw the Dragi preparing to send trade goods to Stonehaven, Maia saw a peaceful stroll through the Rocky Slopes, Jutara saw Dragonborn soldiers (the Dragi) lined up inside Stonehaven, and Geog saw the shadow of a great creature roaring. Gary saw the death of Gunter the goblin to the orcs and was very upset. Breadwig used his wand of sparkles on Gary to cheer him up.
- Magma quickly put his pigeon ring back on and began his flight to the Dragi castle. He thought he may be able to get their aid and would at least stop them from sending men to their doom.
- The others rested, but no one kept watch. They awoke to, two orcs stood over them. Geog reacted quickly blocking a strike to Jutara and Maia used a quick mace to the face to stop the orc about to stab Jatumal.
- Jatumal stabbed the fallen orc who didn't react, it seemed he was already dead. Jutara killed her attacker with her lightning sword
- Arrows flew throw the shadow. One hit Breadwig square in the chest, he jumped behind cover and shouted to the others the direction the arrows had come from.
- Geog rushed around the pond to the attackers, while the others took shots at the orcs who had hidden themselves behind a rock. Most of their attacks bounced off the rock, but Jutara was able to taunt one into sticking their head out and Maia used her sling hitting him right between the eyes and killing him.
- The other orc shot at Geog but missed and before long Geog had chopped his arm off with his axe. The orc collapsed to the floor, blood spewing and Jatumal finished the dying orc off with an arrow.
- Rudely awoken and seeing that the Orcs meant business the party decided to continue towards Stonehaven
- Maia flew up high with her levitating boots and saw a huge orc army of thousands had amassed outside the walls of Stonehaven. The people of Stonehaven would be no match for them in the long run, but were holding them off at the gaps in the ruined walls for now. There were also a few other orc scouting parties dotted around the landscape.
- Breadwig used an invisibility spell to cloak the party and their horses in hopes the orcs would not see them. They then made their way to the secret entrance to the second level of Stonehaven. 
- They arrived without issue thanks to Breadwig's invisibility spell. Gary did the secret knock to get in, which was just a single knock, then Gunter filled them in on the situation. Gary was overjoyed to see Gunter still alive.
- The people of Stonehaven had taken the first and ground level of the dungeon, slowly clearing out the hostile monsters there. They had gathered some other goblins who had agreed to join Stonehaven along the way. They had also barricaded off the lower levels for now. Then seemingly out of nowhere this huge orc army had come. Luckily they had barricaded the gaps in the exterior wall, and were just about holding the orcs off for now.
- People from Stonehaven had also been going missing. The party thought this was likely Pavnutia.
- Gunter walked the party to the frontlines to see how they could help, meanwhile Magma approached the Dragi castle. Still a little dubious about Magma after his exile, the guards didn't let him in initially, but eventually they agreed to allow him to have an audience with his father. His father agreed to send the Dragi to fight with Stonehaven as long as Magma would once again take up the mantle of Prince of the Dragi. Magma cautiously agreed initially wanting to provide a son in his place, but his father didn't like the idea yet. Magma was adorned with his heraldic Dragi plate and they agree to set out for Stonehaven at dawn.
### The Frontlines
- Geog worked with the kobolds who had newly joined Stonehaven to make a large bomb, Maia laid traps and barricades around Stonehaven with some other inhabitants. Jatumal and Jutara headed up to the surface to see how they could help on the frontlines. Breadwig followed behind them slightly less enthusiastically.
- At the Frontlines Jatumal and Jutara wanted to try and capture an orc and convince them to explain why they were here. He set up by the archers and charmed an orc with his charm ring, while Jutara and Breadwig went forward and made way to pull the orc out of the struggle.
- They managed to pull the orc out and sat him by a ruined building wall on the inside of the defensive wall. Jutara used her thought reading necklace to find out why the orcs were here.
- It seemed a vampire had called them, likely Pavnutia, but she hadn't amassed quite so large an army. Some other evil in the depths of Stonehell had drawn this much greater force
- The vampire was angry having not had her wishes fulfilled. Like having seen the dead tarrasque and not having and infinite blood supply had upset her
- At that moment Magma dropped from his pigeon form and landed in a superhero landing wearing his heraldic plate. He had flown ahead of the greater dragi force.
- They mounted the defensive wall and rained arrows down upon the incoming horde targeting the larger targets like trolls and ogres. Maia and Geog shortly made their way up to bomb in hand.
- They lined up all the archers with flaming arrows and had Maia use her levitating boots to drop the kobold sized bomb on the orcs. The archers then attempted to hit the bomb just before it hit the ground to maximise the damage from the explosion. All the archers missed the bomb but still hit other orcs, but Jatumal hit it at the perfect moment clearing hundreds of them from the field along with a few more ogres.
- Maia dodged a few arrows as she levitated taking a glancing hit from an arrow.
- It seemed for every orc that was killed 10 replaced him
- As Maia returned to the wall, gargoyles began flying over the wall. Magma took many out with his fire breath and the others were made short work of by the rest of the party.
- Magma cast a lightning strike through the orcs coming towards one of the breaches cutting a slice through the orc army. The dragi used this to make their way into the wall using their fire breath to hold back the orcs as they went. 
- A fire giant was now quickly approaching and was taller than the wall. It looked like he could break down the wall and end the current stalemate of the attack. Maia flew out with her anti-corruption staff and used it on the giant making him disappear.
- With the immediate threats taken care of, the party gave the Stonehaveners order to retreat to the stairs if they got overwhelmed and to use the traps and barricades that had been laid inside Stonehaven. Then the party made their way inside Stonehaven intending to look for and stop this great evil in the depths of Stonehell and maybe find Pavnutia along the way.
### Into the Heart of Hell
- The goblins guided the party to the barricades they had used to stop the monsters from the lower levels to get up
- They pulled the barricades aside to allow the party through and told them the secret knock of just knocking once
- The party then began working their way down into the depth of Stonehell
- Jutara, with her chaotic nature, felt the pull of the evil somewhat like the orcs had. Maybe the reason the party had been saw drawn to the place wasn't so different from these other evils
- On the section of the 3rd floor they explored they found an area that seemed to have been carved by dwarves. There were dwarven statues, writing and carvings.
- The party found an old dwarven chapel, moved an altar and descended into its secret basement by solving a riddle to move the altar aside.
- In the basement they fought a giant tarantula and a wraith, finding great treasure and a dwarven warhammer called lightbringer
- Magma and Geog argued about who should have the hammer. Maia also wanted it and got bored of their arguing so took the hammer before they could
- They also found a group of kobolds renovating part of the dungeon. They had found a deteriorating part of the walls that they were repairing. Jutara tried to kill their leader but Maia blocked her and they turned out to be quite friendly 
- Following the draw of evil Jutara led the part to a room where three ogres were arguing about if elf meat tasted good. Jatumal was not happy that one didn't think elf meat tasted good, jumped on his back and tried to slit his throat
- The ogre took heavy damage but had thick skin and tried to bite Jatumal's head off but missed
- The other ogres than began trying to take Jatumal from him and eat Jatumal. While the others watched from a nearby corridor 
- When the time was right the other party members jumped in. Jutara hit one with her soul splitting sword, its ghost was confused and began laughing that it could put its hand through the walls now
- Maia used her new warhammer breaking the arm of one of the other ogres, while Jatumal was trying to bite the ogre that still had him in their hand
- The ogres were clumsy and slow to react, missing many attacks with their large clubs, and the party used this making short work of the remaining ogres
- The ghost ogre decided he should go and tell his friends about being a ghost and walked off down the corridor. The party quickly made their way down some nearby stairs before the other ogres came
- Jatumal rode his shield down the stairs knocking over some neanderthal like men at the bottom of the stairs 
- They were very confused and spoke in broken language. It seemed they had been heading out for a hunt. The party convinced the men to take them to their camp
- They snaked through what moved from brick walls to a cave system. The walls were lined with glowing snail trails
- After a while they were taken to the camp. It seemed the neanderthals kept slaves, made them fight in a pit and also ate any meat from the dungeon including other humans
- They were led past a larger wicker man made of fungus and other cave vegetation where men gathered to go on a hunt and then arrived at the sleeping area of the camp
- The chief welcomed them and then they slept in some spare rolls of fur in the camp which the neanderthals said came from apes and lions. They were aware of the risks but were also outnumbered and the people seemed friendly so the slept there anyway
