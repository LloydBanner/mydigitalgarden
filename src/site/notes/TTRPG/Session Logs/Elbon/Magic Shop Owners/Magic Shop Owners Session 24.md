## Session Notes

**[[TTRPG/Elbon Setting/Calendar/Elbon Calendar\|Calendar]] Date:** 47-3-596

[AI Podcast Overview](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1TL0_VQqHJilXJxvwBBKib8Rtm82k-Bdv/view?usp=sharing) (From [[Sources/Gemini Notebook\|Gemini Notebook]])
### Going Deeper
- The party awoke to see the tent they had slept in was made of skin
- The Berserker Chief and a few of his elites were sat watching them sleep. They had much better gear than the ones who had led the party here
- One of the elites liked their lips, but the chief said no
- In broken common he asked the party, who looked strong, to join their hunt
- The party agreed partially out of fear of being eaten and hoping that maybe they could convince the Berserkers to fight the great evil below
- The berserkers seemed scared of the great evil and didn't travel too far from their floor
- All the berserkers and the party gathered around the wicker man made of fungi and roots
- The berserkers began chanting and Jatumal and Geog and some of the other party members joined in. "Hunt, hunt, hunt," they chanted
- After they whipped themself up into a rage they began charging off in different directions
- Jutara encouraged them to follow an elite that was heading towards the draw of the great evil below
- Along the way they encountered a group of Kobolds that began to flee in terror. The berserkers slew many as they ran and Jutara, Magma and Geog joined in. Geog completely destroyed on by stamping on him while the other two shot them down with arrows
- Uncomfortable with the slaughter the others stayed back. Maia and Jatumal were particularly unhappy about this
- They continued running through the cave tunnels, along the way Maia slipped, but was not badly injured and they encountered an ochre jelly that looked to be in the process of splitting
- The party were all much happier to fight this as they knew if it was left to multiply it could become dangerous later. The berserkers ignored it and carried on to other areas in their rage
- Magma tried to attack with his ice sword, Chillrend, but dropped it into the jelly, Geog attacked with Darkfire burning the slime, but causing it to split. Breadwig threw his lantern on the two smaller jellies and they went up in flame and disintegrated into piles of goo
- Jutara added the jelly goo to her cup which had the demon sludge and other sludges she had collected 
- The party followed Jutara towards the evil
- They found a room with a hatch in the middle and lots of discarded debris
- The hatch led to a hole which had a platform with a mechanism attached at the bottom
- Jatumal began firing arrows at the mechanism. It took a few shots but he was able to activate it and the platform raised up. It was a lift
- The party gathered on the platform and Smuv tried to work the controls, electrocuted themself in the process, and managed to lower the platform back down
- They found themselves in a much cleaner and more ornate hallway at the bottom of the lift
- They started exploring the hallway and followed Juatara's lure to the evil. Along the way a snake cautiously followed them. Jatumal shot it, a few of the others flailed at the snake and missed its slivering body, Breadwig accidentally threw an ignid tooth dagger backwards at Magma, and then Geog and Maia finished it off.
- They soon found themselves in mine tunnels with broken tracks that intersected the more ornate corridors
- After following these tunnels for a while they entered a room with old miners bunks, broken tools and three mountain trolls looting
- The mountain trolls didn't notice them initially, but then Jutara whistled at them. She tried to seduce them unsuccessfully and instead they were just confused
- The party and the trolls talked for a while. From the conversation the party found out that Pavnutia was in the great hall of the trolls trying to convince the Jarl to send his trolls up to attack Stonehaven from below. These trolls also didn't seem to like Pavnutia much. They allowed the party to loot them room and agreed to help the party stop Pavnutia the vampire
- In the room the party found a fair quantity of gold nuggets
- The trolls led the party to their halls where they were keeping livestock and other equipment
- On the way in the party were led through a barracks of sorts where there were another 4 trolls. They asked where they were going. The first three trolls said to stop the vampire, but the 4 didn't like this. The troll jarl had agreed to help the vampire now and they were feasting in the great hall. 
- An argument between the trolls ensued. Breadwig tried to walk past, two of the 4 stepped in the way of the door, but Breadwig claimed to need the toilet and they let him past. The rest of the party also claimed to need the toilet and each got through while the arguing continued
- The party found themselves in a long corridor with a well and two human slaves were fixing a wall for the trolls. The party offered to free the slaves if they would help them fight the trolls. They didn't seem up to this and asked if they could free them afterwards. They did however explain more about the feast to the party and tell them of a sealed door the troll jarl had been using to get to the lower levels. In fact they were quite willing to help the party as long as they didn't make them fight anything
- Since the feast was ongoing most of the troll rooms were empty and the part took the opportunity to loot the chief's and the chief's wife's rooms. Inside the rooms found some treasure and a cape of protection.
- They also began breaking the seals on the doors with miners pickaxes they had picked up earlier. This was masked by the noise of the feast
- They broke the seals and opened up a once again ornately carved hallway and set of stairs to the next level down
- The hallway had glowing writing in an unknown text
- At the bottom of the stairs was a locked gate that looked like it had been crudely crafted by the mountain trolls. It took Jatumal 30 minutes to pick the lock and open this door. Wasting time where the trolls and Pavnutia may find that they had sneaked back to get to and stop the great evil
- Behind the gate were eight floating sarcophagi made of an opaque brown glass like material. The party tried tucking them receiving a slight static shock
- The room was covered in dust and from it the party were able to work out where the troll Jarl had been. It seemed he had been inspecting the sarcophagi. The sarcophagi had pillars next to them with the glowing unknown writing on
- The slaves had tagged along since they weren't fighting the trolls and vampire
- The party opened the only other door in the room which was to the west and made of heavy stone. It led to a long empty and incredibly dusty corridor with many pillars with the same glowing script that had been etched into the stairs
- It seemed the trolls had not gotten much further than this, as the dust was almost entirely undisturbed. The part returned to the room with the sarcophagi
- Here they expanded the Kraken, their magic boat, just enough to retrieve the Sarzan painting and went inside it
- They greeted Sarzan. He was very happy to see them, having not been visited for some time. (Sarzan seemed to be lonely) He explained that he had decoded the demon book from Acman and believed he knew a way to create portals to other realms. They may be able to use this to find Gareth's wife.
- The party asked Sarzan about Stonehell. He said it had always drawn evil to it and that he believed something was drawing the evil. He also said there was a mage who had lived in Stonehell called the plated mage. He suspected he could still be around, but explained that he and the plated mage had a heated working relationship
