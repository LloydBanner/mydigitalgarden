I have recently been rereading [[Sources/The Hobbit (book)\|The Hobbit]] and was struck by the way problem are solved as much with violence as they are with quick thinking, compassion, and riddles. There is also a fair share of fighting, Bilbo practically slays a whole colony of spiders on his own, but that isn't the only solution. Now I like a good fight scene but I think we fall back in them because they are easier to write.
